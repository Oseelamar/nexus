01 / MISSION

Intelligence is advancing. Understanding must keep pace.

We study how AI systems learn, reason, and behave. Our goal is to make increasingly capable systems more understandable and reliable.

02 / RESEARCH

Four perspectives. One underlying question.

Understanding intelligence means investigating both what AI can do and how it works.

  1. 01

    AI Safety & Reliability

    Understanding failure, improving robustness, and evaluating how AI behaves beyond controlled conditions.

  2. 02

    Machine Intelligence

    Investigating how models learn, reason, form representations, and generalize.

  3. 03

    Alignment & Interpretability

    Exploring how models work internally and whether their behavior reflects intended objectives.

  4. 04

    Open Science & Evaluation

    Making research more transparent, reproducible, and open to scrutiny.

04 / METHOD

Research is a process of revision.

Every finding creates another question. Every assumption must withstand scrutiny.

  1. 01 Investigate Define the question.
  2. 02 Experiment Test the hypothesis.
  3. 03 Evaluate Examine the evidence.
  4. 04 Refine Revise what we know.

Sequence Feedback — new evidence returns the work to an earlier stage
